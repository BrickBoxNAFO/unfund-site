What the results are

Unfund shows what public sources say about the companies behind products. Every result names its source and date. Results are not accusations, and they are not Unfund's opinion of any company, brand or person.

Our sources include:

  • Russia: the Kyiv School of Economics (KSE) "Leave Russia" database, and the Yale CELI list where KSE has no entry.
  • Trump: US Federal Election Commission (FEC) filings for Trump's 2025 inaugural committee and MAGA Inc.; the White House ballroom donor list published in October 2025; and court records and reliable news reports of lawsuit settlements paid to Donald Trump.
  • Products and ownership: Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts and Wikidata.

Accuracy

We work hard to show only exact, verified matches, and when we're not sure, the app says "Not enough data" rather than guess. Even so:

  • Sources can be out of date. A company's situation may have changed since its source was last updated.
  • Product and ownership data comes from community databases that can contain errors.
  • Some brands have different owners in different countries. Unfund shows the owner for your region where we know about the difference.
  • "No ties found" means no ties were found in our sources. It is not a guarantee that none exist.

If you think a result is wrong, please use "Report a problem" in the app. We review every report and correct errors in data updates.

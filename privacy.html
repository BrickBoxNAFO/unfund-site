Unfund Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Unfund is a free app that shows who owns a product and whether that company has ties to Russia or to Donald Trump, based on public sources. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Unfund has no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and your scan history never leaves your phone.

Who runs Unfund

Unfund is run by the Unfund team. For any question about privacy, contact us at Support@Frogsden.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Scan history. The products you scan or search are saved only on your device so you can see them in History. We never receive them. You can delete them at any time with "Clear history" in Settings, or by deleting the app.
  • Brand data. The database of companies and their ties is built into the app and read on your phone.

What leaves your phone, and why

Unfund only sends data when it needs to, and never sends anything that identifies you personally.

  1. Barcode lookups. When you scan a product the app hasn't seen before, it sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts (and its sister databases Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts) to find the product's name and brand. Like any website, their servers can see your device's IP address when this happens. We don't send anything else. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
  2. Data updates. The app may download an updated version of its brand database from our file host (GitHub). The host can see your IP address when the file is downloaded. No information about you or your scans is sent. See GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  3. Problem reports. If you use "Report a problem", the details you type (the product, what's wrong, and your email address only if you choose to give it) are sent through our form provider, Web3Forms, to our support inbox. We use reports only to fix errors and to reply to you if you asked us to. See the Web3Forms privacy policy: https://web3forms.com/privacy
  4. Donation link. The optional UNITED24 button opens UNITED24's official website in your phone's browser. Anything you do there is between you and UNITED24 and covered by their own privacy policy. Unfund never sees or handles donations.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone

How long we keep things

  • Scan history: on your phone until you clear it or delete the app.
  • Problem reports: kept for up to 12 months after the issue is resolved, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete any problem report you sent us, or ask what information we hold about you. Email Support@Frogsden.com. If you are in the EU, UK or EEA, you also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Unfund is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.

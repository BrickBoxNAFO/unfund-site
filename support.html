Common questions

Why does a product say "Product not found"? The barcode isn't in Open Food Facts yet. Try searching by brand name instead. Non-food products and local brands are the most likely to be missing.

Why does it say "Not enough data"? We couldn't confirm who owns the brand with certainty, so we don't show a verdict rather than risk a wrong one.

Why does the owner differ between countries? Some brands belong to different companies in different countries. For example, KitKat is made by Nestlé in most countries but by Hershey in the US. Unfund shows the owner for your region and lets you view the other.

Does "No ties found" mean a company is completely clean? It means no ties were found in our sources. See the Disclaimer in the app for details.

Does Unfund collect my data? No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your scan history stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is Unfund connected to UNITED24? No. Unfund is independent. We link to UNITED24 because we believe it is one of the most effective ways to support Ukraine.
